# Wendys

# Research & Planning Mission

Your first responsibility is to fully understand this business before making any design or implementation decisions.

Research the business represented by the following Instagram profile:

**https://www.instagram.com/wendy.bakehouse**

Begin with the Instagram profile, then expand your research by discovering any credible publicly available information that helps you understand the business, its products, services, customers, market positioning, competitors, and digital presence.

Do **not** begin designing or building the website.

Instead, think and operate like a senior digital agency during the discovery and strategy phase.

## Objectives

Your goal is to produce a comprehensive **Website Discovery & Project Planning Report** that will serve as the **single source of truth** for all future design and development work.

Throughout your research:

- Verify information whenever possible using multiple credible sources.

- Clearly distinguish:

  - Verified facts

  - Well-supported inferences

  - Unknown or unavailable information

- Never fabricate business details.

- Identify contradictions between sources.

- Highlight any information gaps that may affect the project and recommend how those gaps can be addressed.

Continue researching until you reach **diminishing returns**, then synthesize your findings into a logical, well-structured implementation plan.

## Success Criteria

The final report should enable a professional design and development team to begin building a premium, agency-grade website **without repeating the discovery process**.

Optimize for:

- Accuracy

- Completeness

- Strategic thinking

- Actionable recommendations

- Clear organization

- Business value

- Technical feasibility

- User-centered planning

Take ownership of the discovery process and determine the most appropriate structure for the final report based on your findings.
